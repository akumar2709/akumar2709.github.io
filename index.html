About

I'm a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Massachusetts Amherst, advised by Eugene Bagdasarian. My research focuses on Developing Safe and Secure AI systems with emphasis on robustness, privacy, security, and evaluation. Previously, I completed a B.S. in Computer Engineering and an M.S. in Artificial Intelligence at Saint Louis University, advised by Reza Tourani.
